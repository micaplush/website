title: Pronouns
---

This page lists pronouns I'm cool with in the languages I speak. As a general
rule of thumb for languages not listed here:

- If there's widely used gender neutral pronouns (such as they/them in English),
  use those.
- Otherwise say whatever you would say to a woman you don't know the pronouns
  of.

## English

Singular "they/them", so they/them/their/theirs/themself. The plural reflexive
"themselves" is also okay.

Occasionally I will also just prefer "she/her", it kinda depends on the
day-to-day mood but "they/them" always works.
